# Original pickup and score effects

Reference: installed Dutch `RcHcGame.dat`, SHA-256
`e30781fcdc665d1f217c1a3353761c96e1ec3566f1ad472bb1a8a499cb29dba5`.
Addresses are executable virtual addresses. Disassembly and working notes are
preserved in `current_work/`, including `pickup-native-findings.md`.

## Artwork and animation

The importer combines the original 64×64 RGB artwork with its separate alpha
mask without resampling. `score0` through `score4` display **5, 15, 25, 50, 75**.
The source hashes are recorded in `assets/effects/manifest.json`.

Pickup score activation (`0x4699f0`, `0x469fb0`) lasts one second. Enemy score
activation (`0x46e9f0`, `0x46efa0`) lasts 1.5 seconds. Both update routines use
normalized progress `p`, factor `f = .1 + .9*p`, half-size `20*f`, upward offset
`75*f`, and opacity `1-f`. The card remains upright and faces the camera in
the horizontal plane, including during cutscenes. It retains the native
half-unit corner offsets and uses normal alpha blending and world depth.

Items emit at their current entity origin, including a moving model's
transform. Enemy cards emit at the defeated body's bounds centre (native
`0x49bc80`), captured before its death animation. The renderer refreshes the
actor transform first so a same-frame scripted move cannot leave the score
at the previous position. Enemy purple smoke remains its separate effect.

## Pickup pinwheel

`CRcPickUpEffect` (`0x4689a0`, `0x469770`) creates five particles using
`kaboom2.bmp` / `kaboom2_a.bmp`. Their one-second orbit shrinks from radius
30 to zero, turns at 12.5 radians/second, and starts each particle 72 degrees
apart. The endpoints lie 0.1 unit below the item origin.

The effect prepares four vertices but submits **three** per spoke
(`0x4692e5`). Each spoke has an outer half-width of 10 and a centre half-width
of 2.5. The renderer keeps the unused half-triangle degenerate and converts
the native top-down UVs for the imported texture. The pinwheel is horizontal,
not a set of camera-facing spark sprites.

RGB starts at `(20,255,20)` and changes one channel at a time at 700 units/sec:
R up, G down, B up, R down, G up, B down, bounded by 20 and 255. Opacity starts
at 100%; after 0.1 seconds it interpolates toward 10% over 1.1 seconds. The
one-second particle lifetime ends before this fade reaches its target.

The remake samples this motion and colour cycle from elapsed game time.
Native frame updates discard colour-ramp overshoot; continuous sampling
avoids that small frame-rate dependence and restores saves deterministically.
The reconstruction is based on executable evidence; no native video capture
comparison is claimed.

## Lifecycle and verification

Gameplay records the effect once when a pickup awards its points or an enemy
is defeated. Healing hearts also collect and award their normal points at full
health, with healing capped at maximum. Nonlethal hits, disabled pickups,
duplicate collection/death calls and the zero-score witch do not spawn
misleading counters. Unsupported custom score amounts have no matching native
card.

Records expire on the gameplay clock, continue through dialogue, pause with
the game, and survive save/load with their existing age. Older saves need no
migration. A 128-record bound prevents unbounded bursts; rendering batches
support all five pickup spokes per record and release with the world.

Focused checks:

```sh
node --test tests/reward-effects.test.mjs
node tests/reward-effects-scenes.mjs
```

The unit checks cover native values, geometry, colours, lifetimes, duplicate
prevention, transforms, saved state and expiry. The scene check verifies
visible pickup and enemy counters, pause/restore, expiry and a same-frame
enemy move using the actual renderer. Reports, screenshots and the persistent
browser profile stay in `current_work/`; each visual run gets a new folder.
No release builds are generated by these checks.
